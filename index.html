01 · About

Who I Am

Ever watched a construction decision go wrong? Not just "oops" wrong. Millions-in-budget, years-of-delays wrong. I'm a Construction student at Wits, and early on I noticed something — a lot of site decisions still come down to experience and gut feel. Which works, until it spectacularly doesn't.

That 50/50 feeling pulled me into data. I got curious about moving from "I think this will work" to "the data says otherwise, and here's why."

I still love the core of construction — being on site, watching drawings become real things you can stand on. But now I'm equally interested in what happens before that: predicting risks, planning smarter, and replacing guesswork with evidence.

Through site exposure at Azra Developments and Priotech Projects, I've worked alongside engineers, quantity surveyors, and project managers in real environments. To back that up, I completed the IBM Data Analytics Certificate and I'm building in Python, SQL, and data workflows.

The goal: roles where construction knowledge and data thinking work together — in project controls, planning, and digital construction environments.
